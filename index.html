A Little Appreciation for You ♡

Find a cosy spot. Take your time. ♡

First of all, HAPPY BIRTHDAY! ❤️🎀

I honestly don't know where to begin, because there are so many little things I could write about you. But I guess birthdays are the perfect opportunity to appreciate people, and I didn't want to let yours pass without writing something a little more meaningful than just a simple birthday wish. 🫶🏻💗

It's been around one to two years since we first met at guitar class. Funny how two people can meet in such an ordinary place and somehow end up sharing so many memories. We somehow became part of the same group, and that group eventually became the source of so many fun moments. Sitting together, practising songs, struggling with Tabs, sharing random Teas, Uno khelna, classes ke baad pani-puri wali party ya china town. Enjoying those little moments made those classes much more memorable than just learning guitar. I still remember how comfortable and fun it felt to sit together and have fun. Those seemingly ordinary moments have become some of my favourite memories

One thing I've always appreciated about you is how you seem to have an interest in almost everything. You're genuinely trying to be a jack of all trades, and honestly, that's something I admire. From playing guitar, having such a good sense of aesthetics, and making reels to drawing, crocheting, creating walls arts, doing mehendi, studying well and there's always something more you're exploring. You have this creative side that finds expression in so many different ways. And, of course, your love for going out for photoshoots, and capturing pictures for social media deserves a special mention! ❤️

01 / 05

But there's something beyond all these talents that I've noticed about you. You seem to have a way of keeping your emotions under control, even when things might not be going well internally. I don't know everything that goes on in your mind, and I won't pretend to, but I hope you never feel that you have to be strong all the time. It's okay to have bad days, feel low and let things out. You don't always have to keep everything together. 🤍

Coming back to our memories, I especially remember how you trusted me and comfortably sat beside me during stage performances or even when Prachi's dad showed up at class asking about the picnic. Haha! These might sound like very small incidents, but I genuinely appreciated that comfort and trust. I hope that trust never wears out, regardless of how frequently we meet or how much time passes.

I still remember that last night on performance day when you announced that you were leaving the class. Honestly, my heart sank💔. I've never been someone who likes losing friends, and the thought that we wouldn't be meeting regularly anymore was difficult to accept. It wasn't just about missing the classes or the group; I also felt that there was still so much left to learn from you. And perhaps that's one of the things I regret most: not getting enough opportunities to spend more time together.

02 / 05

There's also something I want to sincerely apologise for. I'm sorry for all those arguments, unnecessary disagreements and mess-ups from my side. I used to be someone who wanted everything under control, partly because of things I'd experienced in the past. Whenever situations went beyond my expectations, I'd get irritated or angry instead of simply letting things flow. Looking back, I realise that my attitude affected our friendship more than I understood at the time. Some things cannot be undone, and I know that. But I've been learning from those mistakes and trying to become a better person. I wish I had understood earlier that not everything needs to go according to our plans and that sometimes the best thing to do is simply let things happen naturally. I'm genuinely sorry for the times I made things difficult.

Now, there's another thing I want you to know, and this is probably one of the biggest reasons I wanted to write this essay.🥹🤍

03 / 05

I've learned a surprising number of things just by observing you.💗

I've always been the kind of person who observes people around me and picks up little things from them. Everyone has something worth learning, and I believe we unconsciously adopt certain habits, interests and perspectives from the people we spend time with. But I can confidently say that I've picked up quite a lot from you.🤍

You introduced me, indirectly, to a whole different understanding of aesthetics. Things like the way you styled your mobile cover art, your Pinterest boards, Spotify playlists, room decoration, and even the way someone maintains their social media presence started making more sense to me. I found myself paying attention to details I probably wouldn't have noticed earlier. Gradually, I started understanding the thought and creativity behind these little things. I didn't just admire your aesthetics; I actually started following them, taking inspiration from your choices and incorporating some of those ideas into my own life.✨ I worked on improving my appearance and looks, experimented with different hairstyles, paid more attention to my dressing sense and started figuring out what suited me. I also became more conscious of how I presented things around me and how even the smallest details could change the overall look and feel of something. In a way, observing you helped me develop my own sense of aesthetics and gave me the motivation to improve aspects of myself that I hadn't paid much attention to before.

My music taste also deserves some credit to you!💝 Discovering artists and songs like Cigarettes After Sex, Arctic Monkeys, TV Girl and others opened up a different side of music for me. I also learned little things about photography, clicking good pictures, taking snaps, making Instagram stories, maintaining highlights and posts, and presenting a social media profile nicely. I even started my own Instagram influencer account, taking inspiration from the kind of content and aesthetics I had observed through you.🫶🏻

You might not even realise how much influence these little things have had on me, but they genuinely have. And that's something I'll always appreciate.🌹

04 / 05

Now that we don't get to meet as often, I sometimes wish we had more opportunities to spend time together. Not necessarily doing anything extraordinary, but just hanging out, talking, sharing random things, learning from each other and making more memories.🫶🏻 There's still so much I would love to learn, and I guess that's one of the little things I miss.

Anyway, on your birthday, I genuinely wish you all the happiness, success and peace you deserve.❤️ I hope you keep exploring your interests, learning new things, creating beautiful art, playing your favourite songs, taking those aesthetic pictures and becoming an even better version of yourself. I hope you achieve everything you've been working towards, and more importantly, I hope you find happiness in the little things along the way.

And remember, if you ever need help with something, need someone to listen or simply need a friend, I'll always try my best to be there for you. You don't have to hesitate to reach out.❤️🌷

Thank you for being a part of these past couple of years and for unknowingly teaching me so many things along the way. I hope life gives you countless reasons to smile, beautiful experiences to remember and wonderful people to share them with.

Stay creative, stay curious, keep being your wonderfully aesthetic self, and never stop exploring the things you love.

Once again, a very HAPPY BIRTHDAY! May this year be filled with beautiful memories, new adventures, endless creativity and everything your heart wishes for.💝

Keep shining, birthday girl! ♡
